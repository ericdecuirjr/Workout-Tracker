Training rules

Warm up for 8–12 minutes and do explosive movements first. Keep reps crisp, avoid failure, rest 2–3 minutes for demanding sets, and stop power sets when speed drops. Use a low-impact substitute for jumps or bounds if your feet or joints hurt.

Increase resistance only when form and speed remain solid. Weeks 3–4 emphasize strength, Week 5 reduces volume, and Weeks 6–8 emphasize speed. All prescriptions are adjustable to your ability and equipment.
